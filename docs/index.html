Firmware builder · Model:Cycles or Model:Samples · OS 1.13

Modded Cycles Techno Edition

Build the modded firmware right here in your browser, from your own copy of Elektron's official OS file. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file never leaves your computer.

1Pick your machine and get the official OS

Download Model:Cycles OS 1.13 from elektron.se (Support › Downloads) and unzip it if needed. You need the file model-cycles_OS1.13.syx, and also Model:Samples OS 1.13 from elektron.se: model-samples_OS1.13.syx.

Experimental. On a Model:Samples the same Modded Cycles firmware is installed (the Model:Samples becomes a Model:Cycles with these mods), packed inside the Samples' own firmware: its bootloader, startup menu and signature stay Elektron's. Tested on a real Model:Samples through its startup menu over MIDI; installing with Elektron Transfer is not verified yet. Only for a real Model:Samples, not a Model:Cycles running the Samples OS.

2Build the modded firmware

  • model-cycles_OS1.13.syx
  • model-samples_OS1.13.syx
Optional: your own DX sounds (a DX7 bank)

The DX machine (20) plays 32 sounds of its own on COLOR. Choose a DX7 32-voice bank (.syx, about 4 KB, from a DX7, Dexed or a bank you downloaded) and its 32 voices take their place; the screen shows the first three letters of each name. To change the bank later, build and flash again. Start from the built-in sounds as a DX7 bank to edit them in Dexed. Choose the bank before or after dropping the OS file.

3Install it

  1. Back up your projects and samples with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Open Elektron Transfer, connect the Model:Cycles over USB and drop modded-cycles-techno-edition.syx (or …-dx.syx with your DX bank) on it, as for an official OS update.
  3. Wait until the Cycles restarts by itself (1–3 minutes). Do not switch it off during the update.
  1. Back up your projects and samples with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Send modded-cycles-techno-edition_model-samples.syx (or …-dx.syx with your DX bank) to the Model:Samples in one of two ways:
    • Startup menu over MIDI (tested; works whatever OS the Samples runs, so also for a newer version later): connect a MIDI interface's MIDI OUT to the Samples' MIDI IN. Power off, hold FUNC, power on, release, press TRIG 4 (OS UPGRADE): the screen shows READY TO RECEIVE. Send the file to the interface's port with any SysEx tool.
    • Elektron Transfer over USB (not verified yet), only while the Samples still runs its own Model:Samples OS: connect it and drop the file on Transfer, as for an official OS update. If anything goes wrong, the startup menu above brings it back.
  3. Wait until the Samples restarts by itself (1–3 minutes after the transfer). Do not switch it off during the update.

To go back, send Elektron's official OS 1.13 file the same way. Before that, set any track using an added machine (7–27) back to one of the six original machines, so your projects open cleanly on the stock OS.

Once installed, install newer versions through the startup menu (Transfer refuses them). To go back to the Model:Samples OS, send the official model-samples_OS1.13.syx through the startup menu the same way. Before that, set any track using an added machine back to one of the six original machines.

Unofficial firmware. Tested in emulation against the Model:Cycles' own code and played on real hardware, but not made or supported by Elektron. Use it at your own risk. Only the main OS is changed: the bootloader and startup menu stay Elektron's, so the official OS can always be put back.
